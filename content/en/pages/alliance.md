## Byenvini. Bienvenue. Welcome.

The Haiti Libraries Alliance is a community of library workers and stakeholders, inside and outside Haiti, interested in promoting libraries, strengthening librarianship and connecting library people through information sharing, capacity development and collaboration.

The Alliance was established in 2020 in response to a survey of libraries and library stakeholders in Haiti, and began its monthly meetings and e-list in 2021. It is a collaboration between the Mortenson Center for International Library Programs at the University of Illinois and Partners in Literacy Haiti.

## What members get

- Monthly meetings and presentations on Zoom, with recordings for those who cannot attend live.
- The biblio-haiti electronic mailing list.
- Access to the members resource area.
- A community of twelve libraries and growing, sharing what works.

## Membership is free

To join the community or receive the resource access password, contact us and tell us who you are, what you are requesting, and why you are interested in joining.
