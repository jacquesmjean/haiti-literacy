## Our vision

Partners in Literacy Haiti collaborates with the Haitian people, libraries and educational institutions so that reading proficiency and learning success rise together.

## Our mission

We enhance reading through Kreyòl books, technology and training focused on children from birth to eight years old, their families and their teachers, in community libraries, schools and centers.

## Our values

**Partnership.** We partner with organizations that share our vision. We never work alone and we never work around the community.

**Action.** We work to benefit as many children as early as possible. The earliest years are when a brain builds itself.

**Education.** We carry a passion for education and the flexibility to seek new solutions when the old ones stop working.

**Stewardship.** We promote the responsible use of every dollar entrusted to us. Our board is entirely volunteer.

**Literacy equality.** We work to raise the reading ability of the most marginalized children first, not last.

## The United Nations 2030 goals

The UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development sets out seventeen goals for a more just, educated and sustainable world. The UN has named libraries as key partners in this work, because a library gives a community access to information, protects cultural heritage and brings knowledge to people in a form they can hold in their hands. PILH supports this agenda in every village where we work.

Involvement with PILH is open to anyone who wants to help young children become readers and who embraces our vision, mission and values.
