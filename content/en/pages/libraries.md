## What a PILH library strives to be

- High quality, carefully selected resources and tools, in all formats including digital where wi-fi allows.
- Trained library workers who serve children from birth through eight years old and keep learning from library experts.
- Guidance for older children, seven and eight, in finding and using different kinds of information.
- A welcoming, safe, well lit place for evening schoolwork for older girls and boys.
- Programs and activities in Kreyòl first, with French and English books alongside, that include siblings, parents and grandparents where possible, and that spark children's own creativity.
- Teachers and caregivers empowered to expand literacy practice in their classrooms.
- Community leaders engaged, schools in partnership, and NGOs working together.

## Libraries we have mentored

1. Cange Community Library, Centre. All services.
2. Summits Education Library System, about forty primary schools, Centre. Partial services.
3. Ravine Sèche Library Center, Artibonite. Partial services.
4. La Chapelle Community Library, Artibonite. Partial services.
5. Duplan Methodist Community Library, Ouest. All services.

Our primary role is to strengthen rural libraries by targeting and mentoring the characteristics above, then providing partial funds for the ones that matter most. A full development can include library worker training, new and gently used books, computers, wi-fi, shelving, Kreyòl alphabet charts and pre school teacher training.

## Our partners in training

Library workers from partner libraries have carried our training forward, especially when conditions kept PILH personnel from traveling.

- Deschapelles Library. Besly Belizaire, head library worker, trained by Terry Peterson, MLIS, a Sister Cities Essex volunteer.
- La Chapelle Community Library. Fernand Constant, trained by Janet Deavor, MLIS, an HEPI volunteer.
- Cange Library. Trained by Dr. Sara Mansbach, PILH reading specialist.
- Teacher training at La Chapelle by Janet Deavor, Pastor Carlo Belon, Dr. Peter Manuel and Dr. Sara Mansbach.
- Networking presentations with the Mortenson Center for International Library Programs at the University of Illinois, led by its director Dr. Clara M. Chu.

## The Haiti Libraries Alliance

Twelve libraries networking on Zoom, with topics ranging from cataloguing to children's programming. The Alliance is a collaboration between the Mortenson Center and PILH. Membership is free.
