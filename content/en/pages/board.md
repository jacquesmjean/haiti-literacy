## Board of Directors

**Fatima-Iram Din, Executive Director.** A nonprofit leader with more than a decade of experience in community driven initiatives. Born in Pakistan and raised in the Midwest, her immigrant experience shaped her passion for service. After her degree from Michigan State University she built a career in nonprofit leadership focused on strategic partnerships, fundraising and program development. She has championed equitable access to education since relocating to the Pacific Northwest in 2018.

**Lisa Bodenheimer, Board Secretary.** Head of Metadata Services at Clemson University. She became interested in library services in Haiti when she visited the Cange Library in 2009, served on the Steering Committee through the 2010s, and became Board Secretary when the 501(c)(3) was established. She regards her service for PILH as among the highlights of her career. Favorite book: The Water is Wide, by Pat Conroy.

**Dan Linginfelter, Board Treasurer.** A graduate of the Central Academy of Art in Cincinnati, retired after a 42 year career as an advertising art director. Dan drew the Alfabè Kreyòl Mwen alphabet chart. He also serves on the board of another 501(c)(3) working in Central America. Favorite book: The Banquet Years, by Roger Shattuck.

**Dr. Yvan Demosthenes, Board Member.** CEO of HamiltonDemo, an executive search and professional placement firm in Cincinnati. He has served on nonprofit boards for more than fifteen years and is a United Way Nonprofit Board Orientation and Leadership Development graduate. He is committed to improving the lives of young people.

**Rev. Mr. Lee Ashton DMG, Board Member.** Holds degrees from Purdue University, Indiana Wesleyan University and St. Meinrad Seminary and School of Theology. Retired after a 32 year career in engineering, human resources and strategy. His family's roots in Haiti go back decades, and he believes one of the best ways to help Haiti is to improve literacy. Favorite book: The Spirit of Villarosa, by Marc Ashton.

**Dr. Sara Currie Mansbach, Founder and Executive Director Emeritus.** A family literacy specialist with a doctorate in Adult Learning and Reading from Boston University. In 2009, during a Rotary leaders meeting in Haiti, she found a new life purpose in developing book centers in villages where children had never seen a picture book. She founded and led PILH from 2011 to 2025, traveling to Haiti fourteen times. She has won many humanitarian awards, but counts the Haitian librarians, teachers, parents and students she works with as the most meaningful part of her career. Today she encourages dual language writers through Kreyòl and Beyond. Reading is the bridge to equity.

## Staff and partners in training

We employ a paid Haitian American part time Outreach and Training Consultant and a paid part time American Library Training Specialist. An American, Haitian and Haitian American trio of advisors helps us answer difficult questions and plan for impact.

Our training partners have included the Mortenson Center for International Library Programs at the University of Illinois, FOKAL trained facilitator Erick Toussaint, reading specialist Dr. Sara Mansbach, Kreyòl grammarian Pastor Carlo Belon, Dr. Peter Manuel of the Rotary Club of Encounter Bay in Australia, and volunteer librarians Terry Peterson and Janet Deavor.
