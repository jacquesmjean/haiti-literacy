Every library PILH supports has the same secret: a woman who decided the doors would stay open.

## Beverly Stephen, Duplan

Beverly is the lead library worker at the Duplan Children's Library. Before she ever had formal training, she taught herself library skills from the internet. Her goal is to become a librarian, and she is building the library that will train others to do the same.

## Judeline Marechal, Duplan

Judeline joined Duplan as assistant librarian in spring 2023 with one stated goal: improve young children's education. Together with Beverly, she is turning Duplan into a children's library and a training center for the whole country.

## Dr. Beatrice Gaunthier, Duplan

Dr. Gaunthier is a clinical internist. She also chairs the Duplan library committee and reports its progress to PILH. Her leadership is why Duplan belongs to its village.

## Michaëlle Saint-Natus, Ravine Sèche

Michaëlle mentors the Ravine Sèche Reading Center in the Artibonite. In 2019 she volunteered to translate PILH's book collection into Kreyòl, and she shares her expertise with every library that asks. Thanks in part to her, children in a village of 150 houses now stream into the reading center the moment it opens.

## Why it matters

Nearly four in ten Haitian adults cannot read. Every woman in this story started as a girl with a book. The surest way to change Haiti's literacy numbers starts with the girls reading today, and with the women who make sure they can.
