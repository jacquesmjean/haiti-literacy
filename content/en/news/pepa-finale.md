The PEPA Education Agency was created in Haiti by Geraldine Pepe to stimulate a love of reading and a sense of self determination among young Haitians. Geraldine, an avid reader born and raised in Cité Soleil, the largest underserved district of Port-au-Prince, was denied entry to her high school reading club because of the stigma attached to her neighborhood. At seventeen she started her own club. A few years later it grew into the PEPA Education Agency, which now runs reading contests and workshops nationwide. In 2020 she was recognized as one of the twenty five best young leaders in the country.

On June 12, 2022, the PEPA National Read Aloud Contest held its grand finale at the El Rancho Hotel in Pétion-Ville. More than two hundred supporters packed the banquet room alongside the excited contestants and their families. From nearly ninety six original contestants, three winners were chosen, and the first place winner became the PEPA Ambassador, promoting the contest nationally for the year.

As a significant funder of the event, PILH was represented by our Outreach and Training consultant Brulan Jean-Michel, who delivered a keynote. We were proud to stand behind Geraldine, an energetic young leader who keeps championing literacy.
