Pastor Edzaire Paul founded the library in Duplan more than twenty years ago. For two decades it served the village with what it had: a room, a few shelves and a pastor who believed reading mattered.

In 2022 the village asked PILH to help it become more. A signed agreement between PILH and the village governance committee now frames every step of the work.

## Why Duplan

Duplan sits near the center of the country. It is semi rural, relatively secure, and home to a school unique in its region. That combination makes it the right place for a library that does two jobs at once: serve the children of Duplan every day, and train library workers who will go home and serve their own villages.

## The people who run it

Beverly Stephen is the lead library worker. She taught herself library skills from the internet before she ever had formal training, and her goal is to become a librarian. Judeline Marechal joined as assistant librarian in spring 2023 with one stated goal: improve young children's education. Dr. Beatrice Gaunthier, a clinical internist, chairs the library committee and reports progress to PILH.

That is what community ownership looks like. Duplan is run by its village, for its village.

## What comes next

The next step is equipment. Computers and a reliable power source turn a room of books into a library that can train others. A gift of $1,000 provides both.
